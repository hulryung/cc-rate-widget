# v1.11.1

Two corrections to how percentages are presented. No change to what is measured.

---

## The Fable card loses its bar

Anthropic meters the Fable week against a limit of its own — `/status` prints it as **Current week
(Fable)** — but that figure is the one thing Claude Code does *not* pass to its status-line
command, which carries only `five_hour` and `seven_day`.

1.11.0 filled the gap with a self-calibrated stand-in: seven days at the pace of your heaviest
Fable day. Side by side with the real thing it read **31% where `/status` said 79%**. The two
numbers measure unrelated quantities, and putting them in the same shape invited exactly the
comparison that fails.

So the card now shows tokens, cost and the reset time, and no bar. A number without a gauge is
honest about what isn't known; a gauge filled from a different question is not.

## Every percentage names its denominator, on every card

The caption saying what a figure divided by — *Anthropic quota · Max 20x*, *of your typical peak
(4.2M tok)* — only ever rendered on the first card. So the weekly card announced its source while
the cards under it showed bare percentages with nothing to distinguish a quota from a
self-calibrated pace. Every card carries its caption now.
