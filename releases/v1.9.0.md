# v1.9.0

The weekly card now shows a usage bar whether or not you have entered a limit.

---

## A percentage without a number to type in

The weekly window used to need one from you. Anthropic doesn't publish weekly token limits, so
unless you had entered one under **Settings → Usage limits** — most people never do — the app's
headline figure was a bare token count with no bar under it, while the 5-hour card beside it
showed a percentage.

That 5-hour percentage comes from calibrating against your own history: the P90 of your past
5-hour blocks, captioned *of your typical peak*. The weekly window now does the same thing one
unit up. Its denominator is **seven days at the pace of your heaviest whole day**, and the card
says exactly that:

> **67.7M** tok — *of 7 × your peak day (151M tok)*

It is not a quota, and it is labelled so it cannot be mistaken for one.

### What counts as a whole day

- **Today doesn't.** It is still filling.
- **The oldest day in the window doesn't.** The event store keeps 7 days, so that day is cut off
  partway through.

That leaves six candidates, and **three of them must have usage** before any percentage appears —
the same floor the 5-hour peak uses. Below three, the card shows absolute usage only, exactly as
it did before.

Days follow your calendar, not the epoch. An epoch-aligned day begins at 9am in Seoul, which would
split every working day in two, halve the peak and double the percentage that came out of it.

### Why days rather than past weeks

The obvious denominator would be your own past weeks. There aren't any: the event store keeps 7
days and ages everything else out, so there is no previous week to compare against and there never
will be one. Days are the largest whole unit the retained data contains.

## Notes

- **The menu-bar item now reads as a percentage for most people.** It shows the weekly percentage
  where one exists and an abbreviated token count where one doesn't — and now one usually exists.
- **An entered limit still wins.** Set a weekly limit in Settings and that is what you measure
  against, captioned *of N tok limit*. Official usage, where enabled, overrides both with
  Anthropic's own number.
- **Per-model weekly cards are unchanged.** Local logs give no per-family denominator, so
  "Weekly · Opus" and its siblings still show tokens and cost without a bar. Enable official usage
  to get percentages there.
- **Nothing migrates.** The calculation runs over data the app already keeps, so the store version
  is untouched and your events are not re-read.
